Taree trophy questions

Do you deliver trophies to Taree?

Yes. Taree is about 320 km from Sydney by road, and we deliver there with standard shipping at a $9.90 flat rate or express at $25, after 5 working days production.

Can you make rugby league and union trophies for Taree clubs?

Yes. Rugby league, rugby union and soccer are among the most popular categories for Taree, and every item includes free engraving. There is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies to Taree for a presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days, plus delivery to Taree. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15% of the order, capped at $300), also plus delivery. Tell us the event date when you order.

Where is the nearest Alpha Trophies store to Taree?

Our NSW stores are in Sydney: 8/5 Leedham Place, Riverwood NSW 2210 and 51/9 Hoyle Ave, Castle Hill NSW 2154. You do not need to visit; order online and we deliver to Taree.

Is engraving free?

Yes, free engraving on every order.
