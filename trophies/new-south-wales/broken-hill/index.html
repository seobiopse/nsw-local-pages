Broken Hill trophy questions

Do you deliver trophies to Broken Hill?

Yes. Broken Hill is about 1,150 km from Sydney by road, and we deliver there with standard shipping at a $9.90 flat rate or express at $25, after 5 working days production.

Can you make AFL trophies for Broken Hill clubs?

Yes. Afl and cricket are among the most popular categories for Broken Hill, and every item includes free engraving. There is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies to Broken Hill for a presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days, plus delivery to Broken Hill. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15% of the order, capped at $300), also plus delivery. Tell us the event date when you order.

Where is the nearest Alpha Trophies store to Broken Hill?

Our NSW stores are in Sydney: 8/5 Leedham Place, Riverwood NSW 2210 and 51/9 Hoyle Ave, Castle Hill NSW 2154. You do not need to visit; order online and we deliver to Broken Hill.
